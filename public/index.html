MESA Founder's Office · Case 03 · Aangan Studio

An AI receptionist that answers every enquiry — day or night

Aangan Studio was losing roughly half its enquiries to slow replies, and a reply within an hour converts four times better. This voice agent answers the studio phone instantly, qualifies the caller against the founder's own rubric, books a design consultation, and hands the designer a complete brief — without ever quoting a price.

How a call flows

  1. Vaani voice agent (Gemini 3.5 Flash) answers, separates new enquiries from clients and vendors, and gathers the project details.
  2. Qualified callers are offered real free slots and booked straight into the designer's Google Calendar.
  3. After the call, Gemini extracts the lead, a rules engine re-checks it, and a guardrail audit flags any price quote or off-scope advice.
  4. A HubSpot deal is created and the designer gets a Telegram handoff note with an Accept button.
  5. A private dashboard tracks volume, after-hours share, bookings, designer pickup time and cost per booked consultation.

Guardrails

  • Never quotes a price, range or per-sq-ft rate — the studio's own pricing guide forbids it.
  • No design, Vastu or structural advice; off-topic questions are redirected, then the call is closed.
  • Never invents an appointment time; budget and decision-maker doubts are flagged, not pushed.

Stack
